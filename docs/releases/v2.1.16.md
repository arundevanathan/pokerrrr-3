# Dunzo Club v2.1.16

22 November 2026

Promotes the complete user-approved staging iteration following v2.1.15.

Before sitting, occupied players and empty join targets share a consistent seat
layout. Disabled join targets are hidden after sitting. Seven-player spacing and
Omaha overlap leave more room for readable reveal corner indices.

Opponent reveals have explicit dimensions and cannot shrink, avoiding reliance
on intrinsic SVG/flex/grid sizing. Compact NLH rank/suit corners have more space.
Own hole cards retain the same sizing viewport, aspect ratio and overlap during
play and showdown. Suit artwork has explicit red/black gradient shading shared
across platforms, with solid ranks and unique per-card gradient identifiers.

Split-pot results list every winner and amount, sharing identical hand descriptions
once. Compact type, wrapping and expanded result containers remove result scrollers,
including split pots within either run-it-twice board.

No game-rule, accounting, storage schema or namespace changes. Staging was approved
by the user before production promotion. Validation and rollback identifiers are
recorded in the deployment receipt after live verification.
