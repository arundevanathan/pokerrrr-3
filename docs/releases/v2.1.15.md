# Dunzo Club v2.1.15

22 November 2026

Hole cards retain upright rank/suit corners and blank centres, including opponent
reveals. Community cards use a single centre suit and upright corner ranks across
regular boards, run-it-twice, Rabbit Hunt and hand history. Compact runouts reserve
more space for ranks. NLH reveals show two complete cards with a fixed gap; Omaha
reveals expose readable left indices without bottom-corner clutter.

A wider oval felt and revised seven-player rail seating keep boards contained and
separate revealed hands. Showdown chips travel to winners without redundant labels.
Staging practice supports seven players and NLH/PLO4/PLO5 selection.

No game-rule, accounting, storage-schema or namespace changes. Settlement tests add
independent reconciliation coverage without modifying settlement logic.

Validated on staging, including mobile NLH/PLO5 and both runout boards. Production
build, isolation check and multiplayer smoke are required before promotion.
Deployment and rollback identifiers are recorded after live health verification.
