# Dunzo Club v2.1.14

21 November 2026

Use a shared ivory card face across NLH, PLO4/PLO5, community cards, showdown,
run-it-twice and Rabbit Hunt. The top-left index is rank above suit; the upright
bottom-right index is suit above rank, with a large central suit. Omaha cards
remain straight and overlapping. Compact cards use larger corner indices and a
smaller central suit; own hole cards keep their approved proportions.

Settlement images include Dunzo Club branding, the date range, game count,
player results and suggested payment rows, exported as high-resolution PNGs.

Staging was reviewed before promotion. No database migration or game-rule change.
Validation and rollback identifiers are recorded in the deployment receipt.
